# Microflexfilm.com — SEO Playbook (Local + Industry)

Goal: rank top for flexible-packaging terms locally (Riverside / Inland Empire / SoCal) and nationally. The site now covers the on-site technical foundation; **rankings are won by the off-site authority signals below.**

---

## ✅ Done on the site (technical foundation)
- **LocalBusiness structured data** sitewide (NAP, geo, areaServed: Riverside → Inland Empire → Southern California → US, hours, services). *Confirm the hours and add your social URLs to `sameAs` in `layout.tsx`.*
- **FAQ rich-results schema** on `/faq` (eligible for expandable FAQ in search).
- **Article schema** on case studies; per-page metadata + **self-canonicals** on all dynamic pages.
- **Sitemap.xml**, **robots.txt**, branded **OG image**, fast images (AVIF/WebP), Core Web Vitals reporting.
- **Localized content**: "Riverside-made. Shipped nationwide." section targeting local + regional + national terms (no spammy doorway pages).

---

## 🔴 Do these off-site — this is where you win

### 1. Google Business Profile (already claimed — optimize it)
The #1 local ranking factor. In your GBP:
- **Primary category:** "Packaging company" (add secondary: "Manufacturer", "Label printer" if available).
- **Services:** list every format (stand-up pouches, rollstock, shrink sleeves, stick packs, labels…).
- **Service area:** add Riverside, Inland Empire, LA, OC, San Diego.
- **Photos:** real facility, equipment, product, team — add several; refresh monthly.
- **GBP Posts:** post weekly (new capabilities, case studies, offers) — signals activity.
- **Q&A:** seed common questions and answer them.
- **Keep NAP identical** to the site footer: *Microflex Film Corporation · 4130 Garner Rd., Riverside, CA 92501 · 909.360.9066*.
- Add the GBP profile URL to `sameAs` in `layout.tsx`.

### 2. Reviews (heavily weighted locally)
- Ask every happy client for a **Google review** — make it a step in your delivery process. Aim for a steady trickle, not a burst.
- Reply to every review. Volume + recency + responses all matter.

### 3. Citations & directories (NAP consistency)
Get listed (identical NAP) on:
- **Thomasnet** (huge for US manufacturers — high priority), **BBB**, **Yelp**, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect.
- Packaging/industry directories and the SQF supplier registry.
- Inconsistent NAP across the web *hurts* — keep it byte-for-byte identical.

### 4. Backlinks & digital PR (industry authority)
- Pitch your **journal articles** and **case studies** as guest content to CPG/packaging blogs.
- Get listed by partners, co-packers, suppliers, trade associations.
- Local: chamber of commerce, "made in Riverside/California" features, local business press.
- A few quality, relevant links beat dozens of low-quality ones.

### 5. Search Console + Bing
- Submit `https://microflexfilm.com/sitemap.xml` in **Google Search Console** and **Bing Webmaster Tools**.
- Monitor: indexing, top queries, CTR, Core Web Vitals; fix anything flagged.
